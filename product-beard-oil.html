CROFT Hydrating Beard Oil is formulated specifically for the unique texture and needs of facial hair. Unlike body hair, beard hair is wiry, coarse, and grows in varied directions — requiring targeted conditioning agents that penetrate deeply without weighing the hair down.

Two to three drops is all you need. The ultra-lightweight formula spreads evenly, reaching the skin beneath where dryness and flaking start. Vitamin E and panthenol reinforce each hair strand for noticeably softer, shinier results within the first week of use.

Key Actives

  • Argan Oil — Rich in fatty acids and vitamin E, deeply nourishes and adds shine
  • Jojoba Oil — Closely mimics skin's natural sebum, balances moisture
  • Sweet Almond Oil — Softens coarse beard texture and reduces frizz
  • Cedarwood Essential Oil — Natural scent with antibacterial properties
  • Vitamin E (Tocopherol) — Antioxidant protection and strand reinforcement

Full Ingredient List

Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil, Argania Spinosa Kernel Oil, Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil, Cedrus Atlantica Bark Oil, Santalum Album (Sandalwood) Oil, Tocopherol (Vitamin E), Panthenol.

  • After washing your face or showering, pat beard slightly damp (not dripping wet)
  • Dispense 2–3 drops into your palm — more for longer beards
  • Rub palms together and work the oil from roots outward through beard hair
  • Use a beard comb or brush to distribute evenly and style as desired
  • Use once daily — morning after cleansing is ideal

Pro Tip: For extra conditioning, apply a few drops before bed and let it absorb overnight.
