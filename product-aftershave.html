Traditional after-shaves burn because they're loaded with alcohol — a short-term antiseptic that dries skin out and inflames the same tissue it's trying to help. CROFT After-Shave Balm takes a different approach: a water-based, alcohol-free formula that calms and repairs post-shave skin instead of punishing it.

The menthol concentration is precisely calibrated — strong enough for a genuine cooling sensation, mild enough to use daily without sensitizing skin. Allantoin accelerates surface cell repair, reducing visible redness within minutes. Use it after every shave as your skin's recovery step.

Key Actives

  • Menthol (0.5%) — Delivers immediate cooling and soothes post-shave inflammation
  • Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice — Deeply hydrates and calms razor-irritated skin
  • Allantoin — Accelerates skin cell repair and reduces visible redness
  • Witch Hazel (Alcohol-Free) — Natural astringent that tightens pores without drying
  • Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5) — Supports skin barrier recovery and long-term conditioning
  • After shaving, rinse face thoroughly with cool water
  • Pat skin dry gently with a clean towel — do not rub
  • Dispense a small amount of balm into palm
