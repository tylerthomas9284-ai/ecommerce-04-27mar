Most men skip the pre-shave step entirely — and then wonder why they get razor burn. The CROFT Pre-Shave Oil is one product that changes your entire shave experience. Applied to damp skin before your shaving cream, it creates a thin, slick barrier that lets blades glide over the skin rather than dragging across it.

The lightweight castor and grape seed oil base absorbs quickly without leaving residue on blades. The result is fewer passes required, less irritation, and a noticeably closer finish — especially on coarse or thick beard hair.

Key Actives

  • Castor Oil — Provides slick protective barrier for razor glide
  • Grape Seed Oil — Lightweight base that absorbs fast without clogging pores
  • Sunflower Seed Oil — Softens beard hair and supports skin elasticity
  • Vitamin E — Protects skin from razor micro-abrasion and soothes irritation
  • Eucalyptus Essential Oil — Cooling, antibacterial, and refreshing pre-shave scent

Full: Ricinus Communis (Castor) Seed Oil, Vitis Vinifera (Grape) Seed Oil, Helianthus Annuus Seed Oil, Tocopherol, Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil, Lavandula Angustifolia (Lavender) Oil.

  • After cleansing, wet face with warm water and leave damp
  • Apply 1–2 pumps of Pre-Shave Oil to palm, rub together, massage into beard area
  • Apply shaving cream or gel on top as normal
  • Shave as usual — you'll immediately notice less drag and more glide
  • Rinse thoroughly with cool water after shaving
  • Follow with CROFT Cooling After-Shave Balm
