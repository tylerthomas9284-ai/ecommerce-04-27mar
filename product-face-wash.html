The CROFT Charcoal Deep Cleanse Face Wash is engineered for men who take their skin seriously. Activated charcoal acts like a magnet, attracting and binding to dirt, oil, and toxins deep within the pores — then washing them all away.

Unlike harsh foaming cleansers that strip your skin dry, our formula maintains your skin's natural pH balance with a gentle surfactant blend. Kaolin clay absorbs excess sebum while aloe vera and glycerin lock in moisture. The result: clean skin that doesn't feel tight or irritated.

Ideal as the first step in the Croft Ritual — use morning and evening before applying serums or moisturizer.

Key Actives

  • Activated Charcoal (2%) — Draws out impurities and absorbs excess oil without over-drying
  • Kaolin Clay — Gently absorbs sebum and smooths skin texture
  • Aloe Vera Leaf Juice — Soothes and hydrates post-cleanse
  • Glycerin — Humectant that prevents moisture loss
  • Tea Tree Leaf Oil (0.5%) — Natural antibacterial support for blemish-prone skin

Full Ingredient List

Water, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Sodium Lauroyl Methyl Isethionate, Activated Charcoal Powder, Kaolin, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice, Glycerin, Melaleuca Alternifolia (Tea Tree) Leaf Oil, Panthenol, Niacinamide, Citric Acid, Sodium Benzoate, Potassium Sorbate.

  • Wet face thoroughly with warm water
  • Squeeze a small amount (about the size of a nickel) onto palms
  • Work into a lather and massage onto face in circular motions for 30–60 seconds
  • Pay extra attention to the T-zone and any congested areas
  • Rinse thoroughly with cool water to close pores
  • Pat dry with a clean towel — do not rub
  • Follow immediately with CROFT Overnight Recovery Serum or Daily Moisturizer SPF 30

Frequency: Use morning and night for best results. If skin feels overly dry, reduce to once daily.
