The CROFT Overnight Recovery Serum is the most advanced product in the lineup — a targeted treatment serum for men who want measurable anti-aging results without a complicated routine. Apply it after cleansing at night, let it absorb, and your skin does the rest.

Retinol at 0.3% is the clinically established sweet spot — effective enough to accelerate cell turnover and stimulate collagen production, gentle enough for nightly use without excessive dryness or peeling. Backed by a peptide complex that firms and plumps, and ceramides that repair the moisture barrier. After 4 weeks, the difference is visible.

Key Actives

  • Retinol (0.3%) — Accelerates cell turnover, reduces fine lines and hyperpigmentation
  • Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1 & Tetrapeptide-7 — Peptide complex that boosts collagen and firms skin
  • Ceramide NP, AP, EOP — Three-ceramide blend that restores and strengthens the skin barrier
  • Hyaluronic Acid (High + Low MW) — Dual-weight hydration that penetrates deeply and plumps
  • Niacinamide (4%) — Fades dark spots and minimizes pore appearance overnight
  • Use at night only — retinol increases sun sensitivity during the day
  • After cleansing with CROFT Face Wash, pat skin dry
  • Dispense 3–4 drops onto fingertips
  • Press gently into skin — do not rub vigorously
  • Allow 5 minutes to fully absorb before sleep
  • Always apply Daily Moisturizer SPF 30 the following morning

Start slow: Use every other night for the first 2 weeks, then increase to nightly as tolerated.
