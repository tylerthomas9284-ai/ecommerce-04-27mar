Common Questions

FAQ

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days within the continental US. Expedited options (2-day and overnight) are available at checkout. All orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. If you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, contact us at hello@croftgrooming.site and we'll arrange a full refund or exchange — no questions asked.

Are CROFT products safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. All CROFT products are formulated without parabens, sulfates, artificial fragrances, or other common irritants. Each product is dermatologically tested. However, we always recommend a patch test before first use if you have known sensitivities.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number on the carrier's website (UPS or USPS depending on your location) to track your package in real time.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?

Yes — we work with barbershops, spas, and retail partners across the US. Please reach out via the contact form above selecting "Wholesale / Partnerships" and our B2B team will follow up within 48 hours.

Are CROFT products cruelty-free?

Absolutely. CROFT is fully cruelty-free and never tests on animals at any stage of production. We only work with ingredient suppliers who uphold the same ethical standards.
