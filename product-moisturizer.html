Sun damage is the number one cause of premature skin aging in men — and most men aren't wearing any SPF at all. The CROFT Daily Moisturizer SPF 30 solves both problems at once: it provides all-day hydration while protecting against UVA and UVB rays that cause wrinkles, dark spots, and skin damage.

The formula uses mineral and chemical hybrid UV filters for transparent, streak-free coverage. Hyaluronic acid attracts and holds moisture in the skin, while niacinamide brightens and evens skin tone over time. It layers perfectly under beard oil or after shaving.

Key Actives

  • Zinc Oxide + Octinoxate (SPF 30) — Broad-spectrum UV protection without white cast
  • Hyaluronic Acid — Draws moisture into the skin and holds it there all day
  • Niacinamide (5%) — Reduces redness, minimizes pores, and brightens complexion
  • Squalane — Lightweight emollient that locks in moisture without heaviness
  • Green Tea Extract — Antioxidant protection against environmental stressors

Full Ingredient List

Water, Zinc Oxide (10%), Octinoxate (7.5%), Glycerin, Hyaluronic Acid, Niacinamide, Squalane, Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract, Panthenol, Allantoin, Dimethicone, Cetearyl Alcohol, Phenoxyethanol, Ethylhexylglycerin.

  • Use as the last step in your morning skincare routine
  • After cleansing and applying any serums, dispense a pea-sized amount
  • Warm between fingertips and apply evenly across face and neck
  • Allow 2–3 minutes to fully absorb before applying anything else
  • Reapply every 2 hours if spending extended time outdoors

Note: This moisturizer is designed for morning use. Use CROFT Overnight Recovery Serum at night for maximum skin repair.
