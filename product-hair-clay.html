The CROFT Matte Texture Hair Clay is designed for men who want a polished, styled look without looking like they tried too hard. Unlike wax-heavy pomades that leave hair looking slick and wet, this clay delivers a natural, textured finish that looks intentional but effortless.

The formula works on all hair types — from fine and straight to thick and wavy. A little goes a long way. Apply a dime-sized amount to towel-dried or dry hair and work it through for full-day texture and structure.

Key Actives

  • Kaolin Clay — Primary hold agent, absorbs excess oil and adds texture
  • Beeswax — Flexible hold that keeps hair workable without stiffening
  • Shea Butter — Conditions hair strands and prevents dryness from clay
  • Argan Oil — Adds shine control and keeps hair healthy with regular use
  • Bentonite Clay — Boosts hold strength and adds volume at the roots
  • Start with towel-dried or completely dry hair — clay works best without excess water
  • Scoop a pea-to-dime sized amount (depending on hair length and thickness)
  • Warm between both palms until it softens and becomes pliable
  • Work through hair from roots to tips, then style as desired
  • For more texture, rough up with fingertips. For cleaner lines, use a comb
  • Washes out fully with warm water — no shampoo residue
