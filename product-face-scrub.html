Unclogs Pores & Smooths Texture

The CROFT Volcanic Ash Face Scrub is designed as a weekly deep-clean treatment to complement your daily face wash routine. While your daily cleanser maintains surface cleanliness, the scrub goes further — physically lifting dead skin build-up and helping serums and moisturizers penetrate deeper and work harder.

The volcanic ash used in this formula is sourced from Iceland and processed to a fine, uniform particle size that exfoliates effectively without scratching or micro-tearing the skin. It's balanced with glycerin and aloe vera to leave skin hydrated, not stripped.

Key Actives

  • Volcanic Ash — Draws out deep impurities and excess sebum from pores
  • Walnut Shell Powder (Fine Grade) — Physical exfoliant that resurfaces rough skin texture
  • Glycerin — Moisture-retention agent that prevents post-scrub dryness
  • Aloe Vera Leaf Juice — Calms any sensitivity caused by exfoliation
  • Salicylic Acid (0.5%) — Chemical exfoliant that assists pore-clearing action
  • Use 2–3 times per week — not daily, as over-exfoliation damages the skin barrier
  • Start with a clean, damp face
  • Apply a small amount to fingertips and massage gently onto skin in circular motions
  • Focus on T-zone, nose, and chin where congestion is heaviest
  • Rinse thoroughly with warm water, then splash with cool water to close pores
  • Always follow with Daily Moisturizer SPF 30 to protect freshly exfoliated skin

Note: Do not use on broken skin or active acne lesions. Discontinue use if persistent irritation occurs.
