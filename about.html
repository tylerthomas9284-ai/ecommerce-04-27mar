CROFT started with a simple belief: men deserve grooming products engineered to actually perform — not just look good on a shelf.

How It Started

Frustrated With Mediocrity,
We Built Better

In 2019, Marcus Cole — a former dermatology researcher and amateur boxer — was tired of picking between products that were either chemically harsh or so gentle they did nothing. He believed men were being underserved by an industry full of filler ingredients and inflated claims.

Starting in a small lab in Los Angeles, Marcus formulated every CROFT product from scratch — testing each formula against strict efficacy standards before it ever reached a shelf. No shortcuts. No fillers. Every ingredient earns its place.

Today, CROFT ships to all 50 states and serves over 12,000 customers who refuse to settle for average. The lab is bigger. The team has grown. But the standard hasn't changed one bit.

How We Make It

Lab-Crafted.
Human-Tested.

Every CROFT formula begins in our in-house lab. Our team of cosmetic chemists and dermatology consultants collaborates on each product for an average of 14 months before it's cleared for production.

We run stability tests, patch tests, and real-world trials on volunteer testers across different skin types before any product ships. The result: formulas that are safe, consistent, and genuinely effective.

All CROFT products are manufactured in GMP-certified facilities in Los Angeles under strict quality control. We never offshore production to cut costs.
